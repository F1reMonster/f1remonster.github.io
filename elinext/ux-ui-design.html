User Experience Design

Today, in the highly-competitive world, the experience customers get from the interaction with your business or specific product (user experience) can provide you with a competitive advantage on the market. Here, in Elinext, we help you create products with a great user experience so your business KPIs can grow, your clients stay satisfied and loyal.
With the help of a certain set of principles, methodologies, researches, and analysis, including among others, usability heuristics, personas, empathy maps, CJMs, usability tests, A/B tests, we design clear and easy-to-use products your user will love.

User Interface Design

First users judge how nice, then users judge how wise. This is especially true today when the market offers a huge number of alternatives from which users can select.
At this point, user interface design or visual design becomes a key player and success contributor.
Elinext design team carefully crafts your product so it will provide a great first impression, attract users’ attention to the needed things, make users engaged, and the use of the product pleasant.
This is possible to the strong knowledge and reach experience of our designers in applying, among others, the rules of composition, color palettes, and gestalt principles in design as well as creating design systems.

Websites and Web Apps

Here in Elinext, we design a wide range of websites and web apps in different industries, starting from simple landing pages and corporate websites, ending with large enterprise products.
We individually approach each project and each client, but what stays the same is the passion and professionalism with which our team will craft your website or web app.
Whether it is SaaS App, responsive landing page, or Progressive web app, we design with your business and users in mind. Elinext design team ensures effective process, communication, usability, and aesthetics of your website or web app.

Mobile Apps Design

Mobile Apps Elinext design team is good at creating both cross-platform apps and native iOS and Android apps.
Our designers are ready to create either a consumer or business mobile apps and test them with your targeted audience with the help of quick interactive prototyping.
Working with native apps, we follow Human Interface and Material Design Guidelines still being creative and designing a unique solution that will work specifically for your users and your business.
User-centered approach, cutting edge tools and methodologies we follow as well as well-established communication with Elinext internal teams allows us to provide a great overall digital experience for your users and keep your business metrics high.

Effective processes

Precesses
Elinext reach experience in UI/UX design has resulted in building effective processes of delivering full-cycle design services that cover everything from researches and wireframing to hi-fi mockups and design systems.
1

Discovery

The first step in creating your great product is learning everything about your product, idea, market and industry - the best design solutions are based on data. Depending on each specific case, discovery process can include competitors analysis, brainstorming sessions, researches of the industry; for existing products - expert usability reviews, analyse of the existing materials, including analytical data, or usability tests.

2

Analyse & Scoping

Based on all the information we got during previous discovery & analytical steps we define all the pain points and set business and design goals. We create the list of action items or tasks we will solve during the next design and Improvements stages. Together, we will create priorities, scope, timeline which can be organized into sprints.

3

Design

At this step, we create user experience, low fidelity wireframes, high fidelity mockups, UI Kits, and design systems, as well as clickable prototypes. We follow an iterative approach at this step based on constant feedback from you and, possibly, end-users. This step results in the approved and ready for development mockups. In addition, this stage can include usability or A/B tests and design improvements based on the results of those tests.

4

Delivery

We hand off all the mockups and assets to our developers who make their job and deliver best-in-class products for you and your users. This final step can include Design QA, usability, and A/B test of the developed product.
