PHP Developers
Elinext has been a PHP web development company since its foundation back in 1997. If you need a web portal, or a more complex solution, such as ERP/CRM applications, we are ready to help your company with our top-notch expertise.

All these years, we developed and nailed proven hiring, skill assessment, and KPI systems. There are no 'black cats' among Elinext PHP developers. Each employee is involved in a complex process to prove, maintain and increase his or her expertise.
01.
Step 1
We check and verify the applicants’ background, diplomas, and previous employment. Our HRs perform internal and external reference checks. We may also conduct criminal, credit, immigration, and security checks if that permits local labor law.
02.
Step 2
We provide pre-interview testing and then multi-stage interviews oriented to assess PHP hard skills and soft skills (especially related to communication). The last-stage interviews are conducted by Senior PHP developers.
03.
Step 3
We use KPI measurement to track actual performance against planned objectives. Elinext utilizes KPIs across the entire life cycle of a software project at three levels: Project-level, Team-level, and Developer-level.

Our standard set of Metrics is based on:
- Methodology used (Agile, Scrum, Waterfall, Extreme Programming, SDLC, Feature Driven Development)
- Development time
- Complexity
- Halstead measures
04.
Step 4

We assign 3 managers for medium and large scale projects:


/ 01. a delivery manager who supervises the whole software delivery process and is focused on your project success;

/ 02.a project manager who tracks the fulfillment and quality of the project at the organizational level;

/ 03.a team leader who helps to manage the team at the technical level.

05.
Step 5
We track the productivity indicators according to the action plan at weekly performance reviews with supervisors and team members. On a project management level, the progress is evaluated daily.
06.
Step 6
Elinext supports all the initiatives of developers to increase the level of their technology and operational competence. Hence all the expenditures concerning getting certificates, getting further education, attending courses are covered by Elinext.
