Quick Help

  • You can add up to 5 photos to your identification request.

  • File Size: Each image can be up to a maximum of 5MB.

  • Supported Formats: You can upload images in the following formats: JPEG, PNG, BMP, GIF.

  • Automatic Saving: Once you upload an image, it will be automatically saved and linked to your identification request.

  • Delete Option: You can delete any image after uploading if necessary.

  • Important Details: Ensure the images show important details such as marks or any other context that can aid the expert in better understanding your request.
