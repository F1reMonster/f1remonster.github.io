What are moments?

A "moment" on our platform is the personal history or story behind a asset. It highlights significant experiences or memories connected to the item, creating a unique timeline of interactions or milestones that give it deeper meaning. Moments bring the story of an asset to life, reflecting the user’s journey with it.
