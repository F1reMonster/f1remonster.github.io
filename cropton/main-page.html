FAQ

How does CROPTON work ?

CROPTON allows any human being to join the world of finance and can earn up to 15% per year in US dollars. This is possible via two components: the “trading engine” and deep market analytics.

What is CPR’s price stabilization mechanism?

CRP is backed by 100% real fiat assets without the use of algorithmic stabilization schema. The mechanism is transparent and reliable, the fiat portfolio provides weekly cash flow.

What is an ETF, REIT, OPTIONS?

All these instruments are financial derivatives, corresponding to real assets in the real world, such as REITs. We carefully and lovingly collect the best tools on the planet for you and give you the opportunity to purchase them.

How often are % accrued ?

Cropton offers revenue monthly distributions. You can see your income details and capital performance history in personal account in real-time.

When can I get out ?

It takes 1 day to unlock the assets and redeem the funds
