ABOUT MODELS IN TECH

Models In Tech is a premier promotional modeling and event talent-staffing agency. All of our brand ambassadors are college educated professionals who have earned degrees from such eminent universities as U.C Berkeley, Stanford and UCLA.

Our clients

  • 8+ years in business
  • 2,500+ promo models
  • 5,000+ successful events
  • 15+ us cities

Established in San Francisco – home to the world’s leading tech companies our sole purpose is to represent and promote your brand above and beyond its peers. Tech is one of the world’s most competitive marketplaces, but with our highly trained staff, your brand will be sure to stand out and receive the attention it deserves.

our mission

Attracting attention to your product is only half of our mission. All of our professionals are also carefully trained to use, explain, and demonstrate your product before each and every engagement they attend.
Our team creates a vibrant and informative atmosphere to engage attendees, showcase products, and generate future leads at your event. We don’t just want to show off your product, we want to sell it. We are truly extension of your sales and marketing team!
Take the next, best step toward being seen and selling products by hiring Models In Tech for your upcoming event today.
