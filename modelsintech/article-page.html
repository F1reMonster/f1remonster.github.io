What factors influence the cost of the convention model services

If you read our recent post about the value of Austin convention model services, you know that they bring in new leads, increase brand awareness, run product demonstrations and increase your return on investment for a convention. So what is the convention model services cost in Austin? The short answer: It depends.

There are a few factors that go into the convention model services cost in Austin.

1. Role

One of the main factors of cost is the specific role that you are seeking for the convention model. The event responsibilities are different for each client and each event. Are you looking for a brand ambassador, a host, a product demonstrator or a presenter? Some of these job descriptions require more from the convention models and therefore will cost slightly more. Default link

2. Length of event & other details

Other standard cost factors include the length of the work shift, the location and the day that the models will work. Of course, a full day of brand ambassador work should cost more than a half-day. Holidays may cost more. Simple street team promotions may cost less than conventions and private events. It really depends on what you are looking for from the Austin convention model services.
