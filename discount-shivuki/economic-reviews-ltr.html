16.03.22

Further acceleration in inflation - the interest rate increase in Israel is approaching

  • Inflation continues to surprise upwards, with an increase of 0.7% in the February index and the annual inflation rises to a level of 3.5%
  • In light of the geopolitical developments and the consequences for the markets, we are once again updating the inflation forecast upward, to the level of 4.2% in 2022 and 3.2% a year ahead, when in the middle of the year it will reach an annual rate of 4.5%
  • The publication of the index led to a sharp increase in the inflation expectations inherent in the bond market, in the short-medium term
  • The contracts in the IRS market embody an interest rate of 0.29% for another three months and 1.25% for another year, without a substantial change from the day before the publication of the index
  • We estimate that the Bank of Israel will raise the interest rate in the coming year at least four times (to 1%), depending on the Fed's announcement this evening and, of course, further developments
