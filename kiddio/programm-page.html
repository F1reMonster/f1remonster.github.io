faq

1. KIDDIO classes are "soft skills-based", what does it mean?

It means that every program's curriculum framework focuses on character building, higher order thinking, and interpersonal skills development. Subject-matter knowledge and hard skills are teached through student-centered approach and contribute to soft skills learning as well.

2. What are the benefits of soft skills for my child?

Soft skills are the future-proof shield for your child: they allow to adapt to this ever-changing world, form stronger personality, build healthier relationships. In addition, overall life success depends mostly on people skills, scientists claim. A.Steptoe, J.Wardle. "Life skills, wealth, health, and wellbeing in later life". Physchological and Cognitive Sciences, vol.114(17), 2017, pp.4354-4359.

3. How can KIDDIO classes be useful for me as a parent?

KIDDIO makes parenting easier in many ways. Soft skills development, particularly social-emotional learning, improves relationships within family and contributes to your child's independence building. Our learning programs can perfecly fit to your family's lifestyle and daily schedule: classroom is at your own place, you can choose a comfortable schedule and change it if necessary, you're free to pause studies for any reason up to 3 months. Plus, you can have much needed me-time while your child learns a lot and has fun!

4. What to expect from KIDDIO classes?

Our quality school and preschool enrichment programs are aimed at boosting your child's personality and intelligence, helping them to reach their highest potential. Every program combines the best virtual learning practices along with latest educational trends, such as mindfulness and bite-sized learning. Our techers perfectly keep attention of the young learners and the learning process itself is fun and engaging, therefore minimal parental involvement is required. Your child will learn through play, have a lot of personal attention, and make friends with groupmates!

5. How do KIDDIO classes work?

Our format is live online classes - live meetings over Zoom chat at scheduled times. There is one free trial class in each program, and you're welcome to enroll in each of them. You are free to join our online programs throughout the year, as new groups are launched regularly. Once you've started with us, you're free to change a group, schedule or teacher. Each program includes two 30-minutes classes a week. There are no home assignments but your learner can perform some independent work after art classes if a project wasn't finished during the class.
